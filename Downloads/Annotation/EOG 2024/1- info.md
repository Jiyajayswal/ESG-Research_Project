### EOG 2024 Annotation Summary

EOG Resources’ 2024 Sustainability Report is very well structured and data-rich. Most concepts are strongly supported, with 9 of 11 receiving SUBSTANTIVE_DISCLOSURE. Only S1 and S4 are weaker, both rated PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE with medium confidence.



### S1 — PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE (Medium confidence)

The report identifies key risk types (regulatory, legal, operational, reputational) and physical risks like floods, hurricanes, and extreme temperatures. However, it does not quantify risk impacts or link them to specific time horizons or likelihood/impact levels. Some quantitative figures exist, but they belong to the scenario analysis (S3), not S1.

A key limitation is that risks are described broadly without financial magnitude or structured risk scoring.


### S4 — PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE (Medium confidence)

EOG presents a clear decarbonisation strategy built around reduce, capture, and offset pathways, supported by technologies like CCS, LDAR, and iSense. These show active implementation and forward planning.

However, the report does not include specific capital expenditure, investment breakdowns, or detailed milestone timelines. Because of this missing quantitative backing, the disclosure remains partial rather than substantive.
