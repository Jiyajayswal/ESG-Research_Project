### Info - EOG 2024 annotation 

---
## Summary
EOG Resources' 2024 Sustainability Report is a well-structured, data-rich disclosure. Nine of eleven concepts received SUBSTANTIVE_DISCLOSURE; two (S1 and S4) reached only PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE. All annotations are High confidence except S1 and S4, which are Medium and flagged as calibration items. No concept fell below PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE.

# S1 — PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE (Medium confidence) — Recommended calibration item

EOG names four risk categories (regulatory, legal, operational, reputational) on p. 19 and identifies physical risk types (floods, hurricanes, heat, cold) on p. 17. However, no per-risk magnitude estimates, likelihood × impact framing, or time-horizon assignments per risk type are disclosed. The quantitative risk outputs that exist ($3.30/Boe carbon cost by 2040, $135–175/t CO2 tax) derive from the APS scenario analysis and belong to S3 — assigning them to S1 would be the exact handbook pitfall for this concept. The boundary call between S1 evidence and S3 evidence is the principal difficulty here. Criteria 3 and 4 are the only ones satisfied, yielding PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE.

The key judgment: whether naming four risk category types with a stated monitoring process satisfies criterion 3 at the presence level, given that no risk is individually characterised with a time horizon or magnitude. I credited criterion 3 on the basis that p. 19 states an explicit named monitoring approach ("monitor and assess regulatory, legal, operational, and reputational issues"), but this is borderline.

# S4 — PARTIAL_DISCLOSURE (Medium confidence) — Recommended calibration item

EOG describes a three-pathway decarbonisation framework (reduce/capture/offset) with named active technologies — iSense, LDAR, CCS pilot project commenced 2023, combustion optimisation — and forward-looking investment intent. However, no specific capex figures, percentage contributions from individual technology pathways, or milestone dates beyond the M5 targets are disclosed. 

The key judgment: whether named technology pathways with active operational status (CCS pilot injection operational in 2023; iSense deployed) without accompanying investment figures satisfy criterion 3. I credited criterion 3 because the reduce/capture/offset taxonomy with specific named technologies constitutes an explicit strategy methodology, but the absence of any capex or milestone quantification means criterion 1 is clearly not met. This keeps S4 at PARTIAL.
